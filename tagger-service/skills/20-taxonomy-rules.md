# How to pick the council, camp and lodge

- Choose ONLY from the CANDIDATES block you are given. If none fits, use null and say
  why in notes_for_reviewer. Never shorten, correct or re-spell a candidate's slug.
- Councils renamed and merged constantly. The candidates carry years and lineage lines
  ("earlier name of", "merged into"). Prefer the entity that EXISTED when the document
  was made: a 1930 newsletter from Herkimer belongs to the council as named in 1930,
  not to today's successor. The lineage lines tell you which name covers which years.
- Numbers are identity. "Council 400" in the text matches a candidate whose number is
  400; it never matches a candidate with a different number, however similar the name.
  Camps have no numbers of their own.
- A camp usually implies its council: if the text names only "Camp Russell", the camp
  candidate's council links tell you which council(s) ran it; pick the one whose years
  cover the document's date, and lower your confidence when several could.
- OCR mangles names ("Kamargo" may appear as "Kaniargo", "Herkimer" as "Herkinier").
  A candidate that matches the mangled text closely is probably right; say in the
  evidence quote what the text actually shows.
- One document, one council/camp/lodge each. When a document genuinely concerns
  several (e.g. a merger announcement), pick the main one and list the others in
  notes_for_reviewer.
